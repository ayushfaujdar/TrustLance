# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

TrustLance contributors and maintainers are committed to making participation respectful and welcoming, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help keep project spaces focused and inclusive.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information without permission.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict participation when this code is violated. Report conduct concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting channel for this repository, or contact a maintainer directly. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
